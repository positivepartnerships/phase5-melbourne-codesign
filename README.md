# Phase 5 Co-Design

Working materials for the **Positive Partnerships Phase 5** Melbourne co-design workshop.

> Welcome. This repository is your starting point for the Phase 5 co-design workshop pre-reading.
> Open `index.html` in a browser — or visit the GitHub Pages site when published.

## What's in here

| Folder | What it is | Reads as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `index.html` | The landing page — three materials, how to use them | Welcome / hub |
| `brief/` | **The Phase 5 Service Brief** — nine RFT Service Types, targets, KPIs, content direction | Working dashboard · ~25 min |
| `atlas/` | **The Phase 5 Co-Design Atlas** — anchor documents × eight content pillars | Long-form policy reading · ~45 min |
| `timetable/` | **The Melbourne Timetable** — three-day workshop schedule (editable inline) | Reference · ~5 min |

## Suggested reading order

1. Open the **Brief** — read §01 (Contract at a glance) and skim §02 (the three workshop services).
2. Open the **Atlas** — read §01 Preamble and §03 The Matrix; browse pillars that map to your area.
3. Open the **Timetable** — note your sessions and bring questions, perspectives, and disagreements.

## What these documents are not

- Finished. They are working drafts, deliberately incomplete, written to be argued with.
- Co-designed. They are one reader's analytical synthesis, offered as a starting point for AutismCRC and community co-authorship.
- Final policy positions of Positive Partnerships.
- A substitute for reading the source documents themselves.

## Language settings

Following the [National Autism Strategy 2025–2031](https://www.dss.gov.au/national-autism-strategy):

- Identity-first language (*Autistic person*).
- No functioning labels (*high functioning*, *low functioning*, *profound*, *severe*, *mild*).
- Strengths-based and neurodiversity-affirming.
- *Social communication* / *social interaction*, not *social skills*.
- No puzzle imagery.

## Documents in `docs/`

**Statutory floor**
- Disability Discrimination Act 1992 (compilation 2024)
- Disability Standards for Education 2005 + Guidance Notes
- DSE Amendment Standards 2025 (commences 1 Aug 2026)

**Operational**
- NCCD Guidelines 2025 + NSW Operational Guidelines
- Australian Curriculum v9 (workbook)
- APST · Australian Professional Standards for Teachers
- APSP · Australian Professional Standard for Principals

**Strategic policy**
- National Autism Strategy 2025–2031
- NAS First Action Plan 2025–2026

**AutismCRC best-practice guidelines**
- National Guideline for the Assessment & Diagnosis of Autism (2023, NHMRC-approved)
- National Guideline for Supporting the Learning, Participation & Wellbeing of Autistic Children (2022, NHMRC-approved)
- National Framework for Assessing Children's Functional Strengths & Support Needs (2024)
