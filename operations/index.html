§ Key Performance Indicators

How Phase 5 will be measured.

Two interim KPIs covering the transition period (1 Jul – 31 Dec 2026), and four core KPIs — with eight underlying measures — that take effect from 1 January 2027 once Services 1–8 are designed, tested, and live.

Interim period (1 Jul – 31 Dec 2026) — the Supplier’s progress towards designing and implementing each of the Phase 5 Services so that all are tested, refined, and operational from 1 January 2027. Includes evidence of genuine co-design and agile delivery.
Interim KPIs Service readiness & co-design 1 Jul – 31 Dec 2026
KPI #0.1 Service Readiness

Progress towards Service Readiness

Progress towards the design, development and implementation of Phase 5 Services 1–8.

Minimum performance level All milestones & deliverables in the Transition-in Plan and 2026 Program Schedule achieved on time and to agreed standards; status updates submitted on time; risks reported and contingencies implemented as agreed.
CalculationInterim status updates and evidence of completed implementation milestones.
FrequencyMeasured over the implementation period; reported in Apr–Jun status updates and Q1/Q2 Progress Reports FY26–27.
Data sourceInterim status update, quarterly progress reports, draft Materials, pre-production website, UAT & accessibility test results.
ResponsibilitySupplier
KPI #0.2 Co-design & refinement

Co-design and service refinement

Services, content and resources co-designed with stakeholders and delivered using agile methods — collaboration, user satisfaction, continuous improvement — with participant feedback used to refine Services before full release on 1 Jan 2027.

Minimum performance level User testing of the new program website completed with demonstrated improvements in accessibility & UX; broad stakeholder engagement evidenced; feedback gathered, analysed and (where appropriate) actioned across all trial workshops and training sessions.
CalculationEvidence of stakeholder engagement and service refinement informed by user-testing results.
FrequencyMeasured over the implementation period; reported in interim status update and Progress Reports.
Data sourceSurvey data, collated stakeholder & participant feedback, interim status updates, Q1–Q2 status reports, website user-testing results.
ResponsibilitySupplier
Core KPIs (from 1 Jan 2027) — assessed against four KPIs across eight measures, following the design, testing and delivery of Services 1–8.
KPI #1 Service Effectiveness 3 measures
Measure #1 60% improved

Educator Capability

Increased educator knowledge, skills and confidence to support the educational needs of Autistic school-age students.

Minimum performance level 60% of respondents report improved attitudes, knowledge and skills, with improved results required over time. Sector-led delivery results no more than 10% below face-to-face PL averages.
CalculationChange in pre- & post-survey results to PL participant questions, and/or School Leader reflections.
FrequencySurvey per scheduled training session. Reported biannually, aligned to each Semester’s training.
Data sourceSurvey data per Data, Performance Monitoring & Continuous Improvement Plan.
ResponsibilitySupplier
Measure #2 60% improved

Parent / Carer Capability

Increased knowledge about autism and reasonable adjustments; increased confidence engaging with schools and staff to advocate for children in their care.

Minimum performance level 60% of respondents report improved knowledge and confidence, with improved results required over time.
CalculationSurvey results for parent/carer participant questions on knowledge, skills and confidence.
FrequencySurvey per scheduled training session. Reported biannually.
Data sourceSurvey data per Data, Performance Monitoring & Continuous Improvement Plan.
ResponsibilitySupplier
Measure #3 Priority addressed

Whole School Capability

Whole-school communities show substantial increase in their effective support of Autistic school-age students.

Minimum performance level School Leaders report the service has addressed the priority area(s) for improvement identified in the school’s pre-assessment, with evidence of progress towards agreed goals.
CalculationChange in pre- and post-assessment of priority area(s) identified by participating School Leaders.
FrequencyPre/post surveys from educators, parents, leaders + longer-term follow-up. Case studies biannually.
Data sourceLeader surveys/interviews, case studies, school action plans, implementation logs, outcome data.
ResponsibilitySupplier, with input from participating schools.
KPI #2 Service Quality 1 measure
Measure #4 100% accredited

Quality of Professional Learning

PL Services meet teacher regulatory authority accreditation requirements and formal PL requirements of all Australian State and Territory teacher regulatory authorities.

Minimum performance level All PL sessions, online learning modules and workshops delivered under the program meet formal requirements for accreditation and/or recognition, where applicable.
CalculationProportion of PL sessions, online modules & workshops formally accredited or recognised by State/Territory authorities.
FrequencyReported quarterly under the Contract Material Register.
Data sourceFormal accreditation, approved supplier lists, and equivalent per State/Territory.
ResponsibilitySupplier
KPI #3 Stakeholder Engagement 2 measures
Measure #5 60% positive

Stakeholder Engagement

Regular, proactive and productive engagement with relevant stakeholders (identified as part of Service 7) to ensure Services meet sector and community needs.

Minimum performance level Actions in the Stakeholder, Communications & Promotion Plan are implemented; user feedback is incorporated into final outputs; 60% of planning group representatives report the program is responsive to the needs of their state/territory and sector.
CalculationStakeholder survey responses, agreed actions implemented, contributions to continuous improvement.
FrequencyReported in Q2 2027 (Jan–Jun 2027), then annually from 1 Jul 2027.
Data sourceSurveys, user feedback, documentary evidence.
ResponsibilitySupplier
Measure #6 Capability uplift

Partnership with ACCOs

The Supplier has worked in partnership with Aboriginal Community-Controlled Organisations to build capacity for both the Supplier and the ACCO and ensure workshops, content, resources and delivery are culturally safe.

Minimum performance level Positive engagement and partnership with ACCOs evidenced. At a minimum, partnered ACCOs report the relationship has been positive and has resulted in capability uplift.
CalculationCase report from ACCO against agreed criteria.
FrequencyAnnual.
Data sourceDocumentary evidence, endorsed by partnered ACCOs.
ResponsibilitySupplier
KPI #4 Access & Inclusion 2 measures
Measure #7 Nationally equitable

Equitable Delivery

Demonstrated national, sector-wide delivery, with equitable distribution across all states & territories and across all school sectors.

Minimum performance level Training delivered to all States/Territories commensurate with population, reflecting an appropriate mix of metropolitan, regional and remote locations. Educator participation reflects sectoral distribution; whole-school training reflects the sector split nationally.
CalculationDisaggregated delivery metrics (workshop numbers), excluding Sector-led delivery.
FrequencyDelivery locations agreed in forward schedule; demographics collected ongoing; reported annually.
Data sourceRegistration data in annual data sets.
ResponsibilitySupplier
Measure #8 70% / ICT compliant

Accessible & Culturally Safe Delivery

Services are accessible and inclusive for target audiences.

Minimum performance level Demonstrated compliance with all ICT accessibility requirements. A minimum 70% of survey respondents from key cohorts rate the Services as accessible and culturally safe.
CalculationCompliance with accessibility standards plus participant survey responses (esp. Services 4 & 5).
FrequencyICT accessibility assessed annually (independent); survey data biannual; ICT compliance reported annually.
Data sourceSurvey responses, Compliance Reports, Independent Accessibility Assessment of Website.
ResponsibilitySupplier & Independent Accessibility Assessor
