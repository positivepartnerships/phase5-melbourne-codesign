§ 01 · Professional Learning

Educators — what teachers and school staff said.

Feedback from 2-day, 1-day, online sessional, and whole-school Professional Learning workshops delivered across every state and territory. Educators consistently described the workshops as the most useful PD they had attended — engaging, neurodiversity-affirming, and immediately applicable in the classroom.

§ 03 · Diverse Communities

Parents from CALD communities — first-language workshops, family-first framing.

Workshops delivered in Japanese, Afghani, Vietnamese, and other community languages. Feedback foregrounds the relief of receiving information in mother tongue, the value of culturally attuned facilitators, and intended actions parents will take with their children — especially using the Sensory Planning Tool and creating support plans.
